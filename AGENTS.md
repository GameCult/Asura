# Asura agent doctrine

- `docs/target.md` owns the ends: purpose, invariants, identity/lifecycle/authority,
  scope, and dated operator rulings. The cut map, once written, owns the means.
- The field is the only truth about shape. The tile atlas is a regenerable cache and
  never an authority. Nothing writes the field from tile contents.
- Surface nets lives in CultLib (`GameCult.Geometry`); noise lives in CultMath. Gaps are
  filled in those owners, never with local helpers here.
- Asura persists nothing yet. Adding a persistent kind means adding a row to the
  identity table in `docs/target.md` first.
